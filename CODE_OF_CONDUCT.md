# Kuasar Sandbox Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a professional, welcoming, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role.

## Expected behavior

Community members should:

- discuss technical ideas and decisions with respect;
- give actionable feedback and assume good faith while remaining precise about risks;
- welcome questions and help contributors find the correct repository or documentation;
- acknowledge uncertainty, evidence, prior work, and the contributions of others;
- keep discussions focused and protect private, customer, and security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome; abuse is not.

## Scope

This policy applies in Kuasar Sandbox repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, events, and other spaces where a person represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, lock discussions, or take other proportionate action when this policy is violated. Decisions should consider context, severity, repeated behavior, impact on affected people, and the safety of the community.

Report a concern to the maintainers of the affected repository. Do not publish personal or sensitive evidence in a public issue; request a private contact path with only the minimum non-sensitive context needed. Immediate security vulnerabilities must use the project's private security reporting channel instead.

Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
